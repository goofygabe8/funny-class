## Art & Audio
- Kart and character visuals are procedurally built in-game. Optional GLTF models under `assets/models/` can be dropped in later (see `js/assets.js`); if missing, the procedural models are used.
- Sound effects and music are synthesized with the Web Audio API in `js/audio.js`.
